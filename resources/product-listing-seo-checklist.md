# Product Listing SEO Checklist

Use this checklist as a starting point when preparing or improving a marketplace product listing. Platform rules and category requirements vary, so always verify the current marketplace guidelines.

## Before writing
- [ ] Confirm the correct product, brand, model, variant, and category.
- [ ] Review marketplace category requirements and restricted-product policies.
- [ ] Collect verified specifications, dimensions, materials, included items, and compatibility details.
- [ ] Research relevant customer search terms using permitted research tools and marketplace data.
- [ ] Exclude unsupported claims, competitor brand misuse, and irrelevant keywords.

## Title
- [ ] Put the clearest product name and key differentiator first.
- [ ] Include only relevant attributes such as size, quantity, color, or model.
- [ ] Use natural language and readable capitalization.
- [ ] Follow the marketplace's title-length and formatting rules.
- [ ] Avoid keyword stuffing and promotional claims unless explicitly allowed.

## Key features and description
- [ ] Explain customer benefits using accurate product facts.
- [ ] Include important specifications and compatibility information.
- [ ] Keep claims measurable and verifiable.
- [ ] Make the content easy to scan.
- [ ] Check spelling, grammar, and consistency across variants.

## Images and attributes
- [ ] Use clear, high-resolution images that meet the platform's image rules.
- [ ] Confirm the main image follows category-specific requirements.
- [ ] Complete all relevant structured attributes.
- [ ] Match images, title, description, and selected variation.
- [ ] Verify GTIN/UPC/EAN, SKU, brand, and model data where applicable.

## Final quality check
- [ ] Check for duplicate or conflicting information.
- [ ] Preview the listing on desktop and mobile where possible.
- [ ] Validate variation relationships and parent-child data.
- [ ] Review warnings, suppressed fields, and listing status after upload.
- [ ] Record the date and changes so performance can be compared later.

**Reminder:** This is a general workflow, not a guarantee of ranking or sales. Follow the current rules for the specific marketplace and product category.
